# Snooze Button Roast

One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning.

## The line

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday like a coworker who shows up on his day off just to prove he's the hardest worker in the room.

## Why this line

The fresh angle is the clock as an overeager coworker: it has no shift on Saturday, nothing to do, no one to wake — yet it shows up early anyway, unpaid and unasked, purely to perform diligence. The punchline pays off in the last few words ("hardest worker in the room"), and every word earns its place: the line is sayable in one breath, names the exact moment (6 AM, Saturday), and keeps the image concrete instead of vague morning grumbling.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, offline, stdlib only — the same bytes on every run. The program validates the gate itself before writing: 1–200 characters, a single line, not whitespace-only.
